Crêpes

Description

Thin French pancakes that can be eaten sweet or savory. Serve them with sugar, jam, chocolate spread or lemon juice.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 50 g melted butter
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Put the flour, sugar and salt in a large bowl and make a well in the center.
  2. Add the eggs and start mixing.
  3. Slowly pour in the milk while whisking to avoid lumps.
  4. Add the melted butter and let the batter rest for 1 hour.
  5. Heat a lightly buttered pan and pour a small ladle of batter, tilting the pan to spread it.
  6. Cook for about 1 minute on each side until golden.
